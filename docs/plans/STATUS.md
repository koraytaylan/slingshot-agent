# Plans — roll-up board

One row per Makina plan bundle. Task counts and outcomes mirror each plan's `STATUS.md`.

Registration follows the closed sequential Phase R contract in [README.md](README.md): only one numbered bundle may be registered without successful final integration; Plan 0001 binds the exact reviewed current commit containing its source-digest-matching bundle and descended from its recorded authored base; and every Plan N greater than 0001 binds a validation-base OID exactly equal to Plan N-1's verified final-integration OID after every earlier plan is complete. Phase R refuses gaps, concurrent or later active registrations, incomplete or unverifiable predecessors, a non-full or unequal base OID, and root/plan status disagreement. Task dependencies remain bundle-local.

This repository is the Adobe Experience Manager half of Slingshot. The sibling repository holds the command line and the local daemon that submit work over a versioned transport and follow it; this one holds the agent that receives it, runs it against the author's own repository, and reports what happened. The two are held to one another by `policy/author-agent-transport-contract-1.json` and by the five-field command-contract identity, both reproduced here as committed bytes with committed digests.
