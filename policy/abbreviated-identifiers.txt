# One shortened form per line, reproduced from the sibling repository so that the two halves of one
# product do not disagree about what a name may be called. The checker splits a declared name into
# its words the way Java spells them - by capital letter rather than by underscore - and refuses a
# word on this list wherever it is declared. A standardised external spelling in a wire string, a
# serialised field name, or an interface somebody else declared is never examined at all.
